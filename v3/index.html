04Measured, not claimed

We say estimate until the data earns measure.

Phone-only shot reading is hard, and plenty of apps quote numbers they cannot back up. We do the opposite.

Step 01

Capture both ways

Every validation shot is recorded on the phone and on a real launch monitor at the same time.

Step 02

Compare, publish the gap

The phone estimate is scored against the monitor, so the accuracy we show is earned, not asserted.

Step 03

Say where the ceiling is

Spin is read as tendency and axis, never invented as an exact rpm. The caddy shows its reasoning, not just a number.

Be the caddy golfers actually trust, and do not claim we are there until the numbers back it up.
