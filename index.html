How it works

Every shot, read off your phone.

Continuum reads your swing and ball from ordinary phone video, estimates the shot like a launch monitor, and caddies for you on the course.

1Capture. Computer vision catches the swing and the strike.
2Collect. The ball is tracked frame by frame off the face.
3Estimate. Carry, ball speed and launch, from the flight.
4Caddy. Your club and a landing zone to find your ball.

Honest by design. The flight is a physically-modeled estimate (drag + lift), shown with measured points versus the estimated remainder, distances as ranges, and a confidence oval — never radar-grade precision. Numbers here are illustrative.
