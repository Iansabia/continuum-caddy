03How we validatePhone and launch monitor record the same shots; we publish the gap.+
  1. Capture both ways. Every validation shot is recorded on the phone and on a real launch monitor at the same time.
  2. Compare, publish the gap. The phone estimate is scored against the monitor, so the accuracy we show is earned, not asserted.
  3. Say where the ceiling is. Spin is read as tendency and axis, never invented as an exact rpm. The caddy shows its reasoning, not just a number.

Be the caddy golfers actually trust, and do not claim we are there until the numbers back it up.
