Measured, not claimed

We say estimate until the data earns measure.

Phone-only shot reading is hard, and plenty of apps quote numbers they cannot back up. We do the opposite: every estimate is validated against a real launch monitor by capturing the same shots both ways, so the accuracy we show is earned. Where the physics has a ceiling, we say so. Spin is read as tendency and axis, never invented as an exact rpm. The caddy works the same way: it shows its reasoning, not just a number.

Be the caddy golfers actually trust, and do not claim we are there until the numbers back it up.
